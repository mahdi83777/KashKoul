    Questions

    Before
    you ask.

    What's the minimum booking?

    Two hours for a single room. Half and full days cover the whole floor.

    Can I bring my own crew and camera?

    Yes, always, at no extra cost. Our gear stays available to you either way.

    Do I need a deposit?

    A [—]% deposit holds the slot. Free cancellation up to [48 hours] before; after that the deposit is kept.

    Is there parking?

    [Two] spots in front of the building, more on the street. Loading at the door is fine.

    Can I use the instruments?

    Yes — the Academy's oud, percussion, guitar and keyboard can be added to any booking. Say so when you book so we have them in the room.

    What about food and breaks?

    There's a full kitchen and two balconies. Order in, or ask us — [we have a café down the street].

    How is this related to Kashkoul Academy?

    Same house, same people. The Academy teaches music in these rooms; the Studio rents them out when the lessons are done. Students and teachers get a discount here.
