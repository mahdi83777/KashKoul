How it works

Three steps.
No forms.

Message us

Say which instrument you're curious about — or that you're not sure. WhatsApp is fastest; we answer the same day.

Come for a first lesson

45 minutes on a studio instrument. We listen to what you love, find your level, and play something real before you leave.

Pick your rhythm

Weekly or fortnightly, 45 or 60 minutes. Change instrument later if you want — nobody here is placed in one box.
