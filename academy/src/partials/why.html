Tradition,
still in motion.

كشكول — a kashkoul is the old notebook where you collected verses, songs and ideas from everywhere. That's what the Academy is: a place to collect what you love and learn to play it.

  1. Keep traditions in motion

    Overlooked Arab and Asian musical practices, back in active, contemporary use. Not heritage behind glass — heritage in your hands.

  2. Make rare arts accessible

    Instruments and techniques that are hard to find in one professional, welcoming place. You shouldn't have to travel far or search endlessly.

  3. Build a home for artists

    Stay after the lesson. Practise, listen, meet other players. Become part of something that keeps going.
