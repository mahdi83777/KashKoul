Questions

Before
you ask.

What does Kashkoul offer besides lessons?

A place to stay after the lesson. There's a free library to sit, read and study in, a café, and balconies that look out over the sea. Come early, leave late.

Is the first lesson really free?

Yes, always. 45 minutes on the instrument you're curious about, with no commitment. If you like it, we'll find the days that suit you.

Do I need my own instrument?

No. Come empty-handed: lessons are on the Academy's instruments.

I've never played anything. Is that OK?

That's the best place to start. We teach from complete beginner to advanced, and the first lesson is there to find your level.

Who are the lessons for?

Anyone from 8 to 80. Children, parents, students, and the grandparent who always wanted to learn the oud.

Are lessons one-to-one?

Half of them. Every week has two lessons: one private, one-to-one with your teacher on your instrument, and one music theory class with other students. That's 8 a month: 4 private and 4 theory.

Is there parking?

Yes, and it's free. Park right at the Academy and walk in.
