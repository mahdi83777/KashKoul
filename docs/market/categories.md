# Kashkoul Market — categories

The shop's categories in English and Arabic: **6 categories, 33 sub-categories**, each with its product types. Adapted
on 2026-10-29 from the owner's full category sheet ("Kashkoul Market Categories", Google Sheets) with the owner's
answers:

1. Remove everything that conflicts with how the Market works.
2. Keep empty sub-categories to a minimum; they can be added back when products are found.
3. Short names, without losing the meaning of the main categories.
4. Turntables go in Vinyl & hi-fi; headphones in Studio headphones; stands in Instrument stands.

**Status:** approved 2026-10-29, set up in Shopify 2026-10-30 (`market/store/content.json`, applied by `market/tools/setup-store.mjs`). Sub-categories with no products yet stay out of the menu until they have some.

**How it works in the shop:** the categories and sub-categories are the menu, the category tabs and the photo tiles;
the product types are the **Type** filter. Each sub-category is an automatic collection of its types, so a product
lands in the right place from its type alone, and the owner manages it all from the admin.

**Naming:** short and in sentence case, so names fit on a phone. The parent gives the context: *Accessories →
Oriental* means accessories for oriental instruments. No two product types share a name.

## Changes from the owner's sheet

**Removed (conflict with how the Market works):**

- Second-hand and one-off items: vintage and second-hand records, posters, concert programs, signed items,
  collectibles. The "Posters & Memorabilia" sub-category goes entirely.
- Things that can't be flown: spray paint, varnish, solvent brush cleaners, resin.
- Lithium batteries: wireless systems, styluses.
- Too big or heavy: acoustic and upright pianos, acoustic drum kits, cello, double bass, PA speakers, plaster, canvas
  rolls and stretcher bars, large sheets and rolls.
- Software and plugins (digital licences).

**Merged, to keep empty pages to a minimum:**

- Mijwiz, arghul, kawala, mizmar and duduk → "Ney & oriental winds".
- Riq, daf and bendir → "Riq & frame drums".
- "Live Sound & PA" → its mixers and DI boxes moved to "Recording & mixing".
- "Arabic Music & Maqam" → a type, "Maqam & Arabic music", under "Theory & practice".
- Humidifiers, polish and fretboard care → "Care & cleaning".
- Portable, arranger and oriental keyboards → "Keyboard"; toned paper → "Drawing pads".

**Left out for now, to add back if products turn up:** santur, kamanja / kemençe / rebab, tabl / davul, sagat, qanun
levers and pegs, pickups for oriental instruments, handpan, masking tape and fluid.

**Owner's choices on overlaps:** turntables (DJ ones too) → Vinyl & hi-fi; DJ headphones → Headphones; keyboard
stands → Instrument stands.

**Renamed:** "Orchestral Strings" → "Violin & viola", since cello and double bass are out. "Strings" now always comes
with its instrument (Oriental strings, Guitar & bass strings, Violin strings), so no two types share a name.
