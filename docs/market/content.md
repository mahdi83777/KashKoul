# Kashkoul Market — structure & words

Step 2 of `docs/roadmap/market-1.0.0.md`. **Status: approved 2026-10-15.**

Every page, menu, button and message, in English and Arabic. Once approved, this file is the source for steps
3–5: the text goes into the theme and Shopify's admin exactly as written here. Change wording here first, then
in the store.

- `[brackets]` are gaps for you or the owner to fill in. Nothing in them is invented.
- Arabic is simple standard Arabic, warm and short. Numbers are written with Western digits (1, 2, 3), as prices
  and Shopify show them.
- Tone follows the Academy and Studio: short sentences, plain words, no sales talk.
