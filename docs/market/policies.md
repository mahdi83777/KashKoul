# Kashkoul Market — policies

Shopify's five policies (Settings → Policies), in English and Arabic. They say the same as the help pages (Delivery &
pickup, Returns, How to order) and the numbers in Theme settings → Kashkoul: delivery $5, in stock 3–5 days, on order
2–4 weeks, deposit 30% on orders over $100 with on-order items, returns within 48 hours. Approved 2026-10-16. They're
in `market/store/content.json` (as HTML) and go into Shopify with `node market/tools/setup-store.mjs --only policies`,
the Arabic as the policies' translations. Shopify links them at checkout; the footer links them too. Shopify's
"Automatic management" of the privacy policy is off (Settings → Policies), so ours is used.

## 1. Refund policy · سياسة الاسترجاع

**EN**

If an item arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, tell us on WhatsApp (+961 78 721 968) within 48 hours of
receiving it, with a photo of the item and its box. We'll exchange it or refund you.

- **Damaged:** broken, scratched or not working when it reached you.
- **Not what you ordered:** a different item, size, color or version from your order.

We reply the same day and agree with you on an exchange or a refund. Refunds are paid in cash or by Whish, for the
item and, when we made the mistake, the delivery fee. We arrange the pickup of the item with you.

**Change of mind.** We can't take back items because of a change of mind, since most are ordered from the US
especially for you.

**Deposits.** If we can't get an item you paid a deposit for, we refund the deposit in full, in cash or by Whish.

**No warranty.** We don't offer a warranty beyond this 48-hour check, so please look your order over when it arrives.

the site or order from us, and what we do with it.

**How long we keep it:** for as long as we need it for your orders and for our accounts.

**Your choices:** you can ask us at any time what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, on WhatsApp
(+961 78 721 968) or by email (kashkoulacademy@gmail.com).
